Privacy notice

Last updated 25 October 2026

This is my personal portfolio. I don't sell anything, run ads, or ask you to sign up, and I never ask for your name or email on this site. The only optional data collection is visit statistics, and only if you agree.

Analytics, only with your consent

If you choose Accept in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 so I can see how the portfolio is used. If you choose Decline, or make no choice, nothing from Google Analytics is loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, that counts as Decline.

With your consent, Google Analytics records:

  • which pages and case studies are viewed, and the page that referred you;
  • how far you scroll down a page;
  • which links to other websites are clicked;
  • your browser, device type, screen size and language;
  • your approximate location (country and city), worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.

It sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_941S7NY2SD, which hold a random identifier so repeat visits can be counted. They expire after 13 months. Advertising features, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and I don't link this data to who you are.

Who receives it and how long it is kept

Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC processes the data on my behalf. It may be transferred to and stored in the United States; Google participates in the EU–US, UK and Swiss Data Privacy Framework. Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

Other services the site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you visit and may log it for security.
  • Fonts. The main site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers. No cookies are set for this.
  • Links. LinkedIn and other sites I link to have their own privacy policies once you follow a link.

Your choices and rights

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing stops analytics and deletes the analytics cookies on this site.

Open cookie settings

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to ask what data relates to you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the analytics data is not linked to your identity, I usually can't single out your visits, but I'm glad to help.
